# Product Rules

- Prefer active learning and guided reasoning.
- Recommendations must cite evidence and be explainable.
- Separate observed evidence from AI inference.
- AI cannot arbitrarily mutate critical academic state.
- Verified curriculum/questions/materials require provenance and versioning.
- Student data is private, isolated, and shared with parents/teachers only through authorization and consent.
- Never fabricate academic data, sources, scores, PYQs, or readiness claims.
